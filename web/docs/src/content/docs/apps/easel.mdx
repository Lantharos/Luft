Easel edits pictures and holds a whiteboard. Opening a picture from Rover, or with **Open with**, gives quick edits. The editor has layers, selections and brushes for more, and **New board** starts an endless board for sketches and diagrams.

<Shot name="easel" alt="Easel editing a photo with a text layer" />

## Quick edits

A picture you open from Rover shows on its own, with the kinds of edit along the bottom:

- **Crop** to a free shape, the original's, a square, 16:9, 4:3 or 9:16. **Straighten** levels a tilted horizon, and the buttons beside it rotate and flip.
- **Adjust** light, contrast, colour, warmth and sharpness, or press **Auto**.
- **Mark up** with a pen, arrows, boxes and text.
- **Hide** blurs or blacks out what you drag over, such as a face or a number.
- **Size** makes the picture smaller.

**Done** asks whether to replace the original or save a copy. **Open in editor** takes the picture, with your edits, into the editor.

## Working with layers

The editor's tools are on the left and the current tool's settings are on the right, above the layers. Each layer can be hidden, locked, renamed, made see-through or blended with the layers under it, and the menu beside **Layers** duplicates, merges down and deletes. Drag layers to reorder them. Text stays editable: pick the text tool and click the text.

Easel saves layered work as an `.easel` file, which keeps every layer. **Export** writes PNG, JPEG, WebP or AVIF, at the size and quality you choose.

## Masks and adjustment layers

**Add a mask** in the layer menu hides parts of a layer without erasing them: paint black on the mask to hide and white to show, with the mask's thumbnail picked in the layer list. **Mask from selection** starts from what's selected, and the same menu inverts, turns off, applies and deletes a mask.

The **+** button above the layers adds adjustment layers: curves, levels, hue and saturation, colour balance, black and white, brightness and contrast, exposure and vibrance. They change everything under them without touching any pixels, so double-click one to change it again, mask it to limit where it applies, or hide it. Put layers in a group, from the layer menu, to move, hide and blend them together. **Effects** adds a drop shadow and an outline that follow the layer's shape.

## Filters

The **More** menu has blur, motion blur, sharpen, reduce noise, vignette and pixelate. Each previews on the picture while you move its sliders, and only applies inside the selection when there is one.

## Retouching

The clone stamp and the healing brush copy from another part of the picture: <kbd>Alt</kbd>-click where to copy from, then paint. The healing brush blends what it copies into the colours and light around it. Smudge pushes colour along the stroke, like a finger through wet paint.

## Selecting

The select tool draws rectangles and ellipses, the lasso draws freely, and the magic wand picks similar colours. Hold <kbd>Shift</kbd> to add to a selection and <kbd>Alt</kbd> to take away from it, or choose **Add** or **Subtract**. **Feather** softens its edge. A selection limits painting, filling, filters and moving to what's inside it. **Refine** grows, shrinks, borders or smooths it, **Colour range** selects everything close to the main colour, and **Quick mask**, or <kbd>Q</kbd>, shows the selection as a red overlay you paint with the brush and the eraser.

Hold <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> while dragging a corner with the move tool to distort the picture freely, for perspective and skew. **Rulers** in the **More** menu shows rulers; drag from one to place a guide, and drag a guide back to remove it. Selections, crops and shapes snap to guides and to the picture's edges and middle.

## Painting and drawing

The brush and the eraser follow pen pressure, with size, hardness, opacity and smoothing. The **paint bucket** pours a colour into an area, **Gradient** blends the two colours in the corner along a line or out from a point, and **Shapes** draws boxes, ellipses, lines and arrows. The colour picker takes a colour from the picture; hold <kbd>Alt</kbd> to set the second colour.

Pinch, or hold <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> and scroll, to zoom. Hold <kbd>Space</kbd> and drag, drag with the middle button, or swipe with two fingers to move around.

## The whiteboard

A board has no edges. Draw with the pen or the highlighter, and erase whole strokes with the eraser, or with the eraser end of a pen; a finger moves the board.

- Draw a box, a circle or a line and hold still at the end: it becomes a clean shape.
- Arrows started or ended on a shape stay attached to it when it moves.
- Double-click a shape or a note to write in it.
- Drop or paste pictures onto the board.
- Drag over empty space to select several things, and group them with <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>G</kbd>.

Boards export to PNG, JPEG, WebP, AVIF, SVG and PDF.
