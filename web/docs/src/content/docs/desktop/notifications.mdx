Click the clock on the taskbar, or press <kbd>Super</kbd>+<kbd>N</kbd>, to open the notification center. What's playing sits on top, then your notifications, then a calendar.

<Shot name="notification-center" alt="The notification center with a media player, notifications and the calendar" />

## Notifications

- Notifications are grouped by app, newest first. Each group shows two and folds the rest behind **N more**.
- Click one to open it, or press <kbd>Delete</kbd> or its close button to dismiss it. **Clear all** empties the list.
- Swipe a notification to the right with the mouse, a finger or two fingers on the touchpad to dismiss it. A quick flick is enough, and letting go early puts it back. Banners that pop up can also be swiped down.
- Buttons an app adds appear under the notification. Apps that support it offer **Reply**, which turns into a text field right there.
- Right-click a notification for its app's notification settings.

## What's playing

The player that's playing, or the last one you used, shows its cover, title and artist with previous, play and next. Click it to bring the player forward. It also appears on the lock screen.

## The calendar

A month at a time, with week numbers. Scroll over it to move between months: each notch of the mouse wheel turns one month, and two fingers on the touchpad slide through the weeks and settle on a month when you let go. The arrows do the same. Click the month's name to come back to today. The first day of the week follows your region, or what you set in Settings, under Date & Time.

## Do Not Disturb

Turn it on from [Quick settings](/desktop/quick-settings/), or type `dnd` in Start. Banners stop popping up, and notifications collect quietly in the center.

Each app can be allowed through: in Settings, under Notifications, choose an app and set **Allow during Do Not Disturb** to Never, Urgent only (the default) or Always.

## On the lock screen

The lock screen shows a few notifications from each app, with their text. To hide the text, turn off **Show message content** in Settings, under Notifications, or right-click the notification center. Each app can also be kept off the lock screen entirely.
