Playthings is an extension from [Schelf](/apps/schelf/). It puts a few things on your wallpaper, behind your windows, that respond when you brush past them with the pointer or a finger. Windows always stay in front, and clicking anywhere else on the desktop works as it always does.

| Plaything | What it does |
| --- | --- |
| Lanterns | Swing when you brush past, the faster the further. Hold one and pull it, and it swings on when you let go. Click one to light it up, and again to let it go dim |
| Clouds | Part around the pointer and drift back together. Poke one and it rains a little |
| Campfire | Leans away from the pointer. Click it for a burst of sparks, or wave back and forth beside it to fan it |
| Stars | Brighten and join up as the pointer comes near, and twinkle when you touch them. Flick across them for a shooting star |

A touchscreen works the same as a mouse, with as many fingers as you like, so you can set two lanterns swinging at once.

Settings › Extensions › Playthings switches each kind on and off, and sets how many there are and which part of the desktop they go on. You can also pick the paper of the lanterns, how big everything is, and whether they show on your main display or every display. The tile in [quick settings](/desktop/quick-settings/) switches them all on and off.

## Put them where you like

Choose **Arrange** on that page or in the tile's menu. Things come out in front of your windows with a ring around them, so you can drag them anywhere, with several fingers if you want. A lantern hangs from the top of the display at whatever length you drop it. Press **Done** when you're happy.

Every display keeps its own arrangement, which comes back when you plug that display in again. Things stay clear of the taskbar wherever you keep it, and follow along when a display changes size. **Put everything back** forgets it all.

## When they rest

With **Gentle motion** on, things sway, drift and flicker a little by themselves. That stops whenever nobody could see it: while windows cover them, during full screen, with animations off or reduced, and with the power saver on. With it off, they only move when you touch them. Either way, whatever you set moving comes to rest, and then they use nothing at all. If you ask for less motion, they stay still when you touch them too, and lanterns just light up.
