## Light and dark

Luft starts dark. Choose **Light** or **Dark**, or let **Switch to dark automatically** do it at sunset and sunrise, or at times you choose. The **Dark Style** tile in Quick settings and `dark mode` in Start switch too.

- **Pure black** makes dark backgrounds fully black, which saves power on OLED screens.
- The login and lock screens stay dark in both styles.

## Wallpapers

Luft comes with 35 wallpapers, photographs of fog, dunes, mountains, coasts and skies. Pictures and videos you put in `~/Pictures/Wallpapers` join them, and [Magpie](/apps/magpie/) can set any photo as the wallpaper.

- The light and dark styles each keep their own wallpaper. Luft starts on Fog for dark and Dunes for light.
- When a picture and your display have different shapes, **drag the preview** to choose which part stays on screen. Luft guesses a good spot for each picture by itself.
- **Fit** chooses between Fill, Fit, Center, Stretch, Tile and spanning every display.
- **Videos** play as live wallpapers. On a laptop, you choose whether they keep playing on battery.
- **Rain** falls over the wallpaper, as drizzle, rain or a storm. It lands on the tops of windows and on the taskbar, splashes there and drips off window corners, and windows keep the rain off whatever is under them. It stops while a window covers the wallpaper, animations are off or reduced, or the power saver is on.

## Accent colour

Luft takes an accent colour from the lower part of your wallpaper, where the taskbar sits, and builds a full palette from it, for light and dark, with readable contrast whatever the picture. The desktop and Luft's apps use it together, so a new wallpaper restyles everything at once. Choose **White** instead for a black and white look.

### Matching other apps

**Match other apps to the wallpaper** gives other apps the same colours: GTK and libadwaita apps, Qt and KDE apps, and the Ghostty terminal. Turning it off removes them again. [Wallpaper colours](/reference/appearance/) has the details, including how apps can read the palette themselves.

## App icons

| Style | Looks like |
| --- | --- |
| Tinted | Every icon drawn as a two-tone glyph in one colour, the accent or one you choose, on a soft plate. This is the default |
| Clear | White glyphs on a see-through plate, dark ones in the light style |
| Original | Each app's own icon |

## Taskbar

How Start looks, and the taskbar's look, shape, size and alignment, are in Settings, under **Desktop**. See [the taskbar's options](/desktop/taskbar/#options).

## Text and fonts

- **Fonts** picks the interface and monospace fonts. Luft uses Open Runde and Maple Mono. **All fonts** lists every installed font, and removes ones you added.
- **Text size** goes from 80% to 160%.

## Cursors

Pick a cursor theme and size. **Get more cursors** finds and installs themes from GNOME-Look; themes you installed can be removed again.

## Animations

**Animations** turns animation off entirely, so everything changes at once. To keep animation but take out the movement, use **Reduce motion** in [Accessibility](/personalize/accessibility/).
