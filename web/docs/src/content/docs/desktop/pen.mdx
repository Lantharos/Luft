Luft works with the pen of a 2-in-1 or pen display and with drawing tablets such as a Wacom Intuos. Once one is connected, Settings gets a **Pen** page.

## Resting your hand

You can rest the side of your hand on the screen while you write. For a moment after you lift the pen, touches where your hand rests don't count, so your palm can't tap the taskbar or open apps while you move to the next word. Touches elsewhere on the screen work straight away, a tap right next to where your hand was waits a split second to be sure it's a finger, and a few seconds after you put the pen down, touch is exactly as before.

Tell Luft which hand you write with, so it knows where your hand rests: Settings → Pen → **Writing hand**.

While the pen hovers over the screen or writes, a small dot shows where its tip points, in every app.

## Pressure

**Tip feel** sets how hard you need to press for a full stroke: towards **Soft**, a light touch already draws dark and thick lines; towards **Firm**, you press harder for the same line. **Eraser feel** does the same for the eraser.

## Buttons

Each side button of the pen does what you choose for it in Settings → Pen → **Buttons**. At first, the lower button, closest to the tip, right-clicks, and the upper button erases while you hold it.

| Choice | What happens |
| --- | --- |
| Right click, Middle click | Clicks where the pen points |
| Erase | The pen erases while you hold the button |
| Take a screenshot | Opens the [screenshot](/desktop/screenshots/) tools |
| Open Heft | Opens Heft, when it's installed |
| Open Start | Opens [Start](/desktop/start-and-search/), or closes it again |
| Back, Forward | Go back and forward, as in a browser |
| Switch display | Moves a drawing tablet to the next display |
| Press keys | Presses the keys you record, such as <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+<kbd>Z</kbd> |
| Nothing | The button does nothing |

**Eraser** covers the pen's other end, or an eraser button on pens that have one instead. It erases, or does one of the choices above if the pen's eraser is a button.

## Battery

When the pen reports its battery, Settings → Pen shows the level, and Luft tells you once when it runs low.

## Drawing tablets

A drawing tablet you plug in draws on all your displays at first. In Settings → Pen, choose a tablet to pick the display it draws on. **Keep proportions** leaves part of the tablet unused so circles stay round on a display with a different shape.
