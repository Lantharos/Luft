Flatbed scans paper into documents. It works with scanners on your network and plugged in over USB, and with your computer's camera, and every page comes out straightened and cleaned up, ready to save as a PDF you can search or as pictures.

<Shot name="flatbed" alt="Flatbed with three scanned pages" />

## Scanning

Scanners show up in the sidebar by themselves: most network scanners and all-in-one printers made in the last ten years, Canon printers that scan over the network, and scanners plugged in over USB. Pick one, put a page face down on the glass and press **Scan**. While it scans, the page fills in as it arrives.

If the scanner has a document feeder, choose **Feeder** next to the Scan button and load the pages: Flatbed scans them all in one go, and **Both sides** scans both sides of each sheet when the scanner can.

A scanner that doesn't show up can be added by its address with **Add a scanner** in the sidebar. You'll find the address on the printer's own screen or in your router.

## The camera

Choose **Camera** in the sidebar and press **Take a photo**. Lay the page flat in good light and hold the camera over it; an outline follows the edges of the paper, and the photo you take is turned into a flat, even page. Take as many pages as you like before closing the window.

Photos of pages you took before work the same way: drop them onto the window or use **Open pictures**.

## Pages

Every page is straightened and cut out along the edges of the paper, and its light is evened out, so shadows and the colour of a lamp go away. A smaller paper on a scanner's glass, such as a receipt, is cut out of the scan, and a page that went in slightly turned comes out straight.

- Drag pages to put them in order.
- Hover a page to turn it, remove it or adjust its corners. Adjusting the corners shows the whole picture with the four corners to drag, or **Use the whole picture** to keep everything.
- **Colour**, **Grey** and **Black and white** at the top change how every page looks. Black and white gives the smallest files and the crispest text.

Pages are kept until you remove them, so closing Flatbed doesn't lose a scan you haven't saved yet.

## Saving

**Save** asks for a name and a format:

- **PDF** puts every page in one document. With **Searchable text** on, Flatbed reads the text on every page on your computer, so you can search the PDF and copy its text. **Page size** keeps each page the size it was scanned, or puts every page on A4 or Letter paper.
- **JPG** and **PNG** save each page as a picture. Several pages go into a folder you choose, numbered.

Once it's saved, **Send** sends it to a phone or computer nearby, with the Pigeon extension on, or to another app, such as Heft to write on it. **Show in folder** opens the folder in Rover.

## Settings

- **Fine scans** scans at 600 pixels per inch instead of 300, for small print and photos. Scanning takes longer.
- **Languages to read** are the languages text is read in. Luft comes with Albanian, German, Croatian and English; the languages you use Luft in are picked at first.

## Privacy

Everything happens on your computer: text is read without going online, and the camera is only on while its window is open. Flatbed runs in a sandbox that can reach your network, for network scanners, and USB devices, for scanners plugged in, and reads your Pictures and Downloads for pictures you drop in.
