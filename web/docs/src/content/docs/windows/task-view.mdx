Press <kbd>Super</kbd>+<kbd>Tab</kbd>, or swipe down with four fingers, to see every window on the current workspace laid out side by side, with your workspaces along the bottom.

<Shot name="task-view" alt="All windows, with the workspaces along the bottom" />

- **Click a window** to switch to it. Middle-click it, press <kbd>Delete</kbd>, or use its close button to close it.
- **Click a workspace** at the bottom to switch to it. Each one is a live picture, boards included.
- **Drag a window onto a workspace** to move it there.
- **Click the background**, press <kbd>Esc</kbd>, or swipe up with four fingers to go back.

It opens on the display the pointer is on.

## Switching with Alt+Tab

<kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>Tab</kbd> is the quicker switcher: hold <kbd>Alt</kbd> and press <kbd>Tab</kbd> to step through the windows on this workspace, then let go. <kbd>Alt</kbd>+<kbd>`</kbd> steps through the windows of the app you're using.
