Sign in to a cloud service once in Settings, under **Accounts**, and its files appear as a folder in `~/Cloud`, next to your other drives in [Rover](/apps/rover/). Every app and every file dialog sees it like any other folder.


| Service | How you sign in |
| --- | --- |
| iCloud Drive | Your Apple Account email and password, then the code Apple shows on your iPhone, iPad or Mac, or texts to your phone |
| OneDrive | In your browser, with a personal, work or school Microsoft account |
| Dropbox | In your browser |
| Nextcloud | The server's address, then in your browser, or with a user name and app password |
| WebDAV | The server's address, user name and password |

Passwords and sign-in tokens are kept in [Luft Keyring](/files/keyring-and-passkeys/) and never written to a file. Removing an account removes its keyring entry and the copies on this computer; the files stay in the cloud.

## Files on demand

Nothing downloads until you open it. Opened files stay for a day, up to 5 GB, and are cleared sooner when the disk runs low, so a large cloud drive doesn't fill yours.

**Keep on this device**, on a file or folder in Rover, keeps it on your computer. Kept files open without a connection, and changes go both ways: within a few seconds of a change here, and every five minutes otherwise. **Free up space** turns it back into a file that downloads when opened; anything changed here is sent up first.

Rover marks each file:

| Mark | Means |
| --- | --- |
| Cloud | Online only; it downloads when opened |
| Check | On this device |
| Arrows | Syncing |

When the computer starts without a connection, the drive shows only kept files until it's back online.

Deleting something in a cloud drive sends it to the service's own trash, such as iCloud's Recently Deleted or OneDrive's recycle bin, so restore it from there rather than from Rover's Trash.

## When the same file changes in two places

If a kept file changes on this computer and in the cloud before they meet, both versions are kept. The newer one keeps the name, and the other gets one like `Report (copy from OneDrive).docx` or `Report (copy from this device).docx`. A notification says so, and Rover shows a note in the drive until you dismiss it or remove the copy.

## Pausing

Syncing in the background pauses on a metered connection, such as a phone's hotspot, and when the battery is at 20% or lower and not charging. Opening a file still downloads it, and changes made meanwhile go up once the pause ends. Settings shows why a drive is paused.

## Sharing

Right-click a file or folder in a OneDrive, Dropbox or Nextcloud drive:

- **Copy share link** makes a link anyone with it can open.
- **Open in browser** shows it on the service's website.

## iCloud Drive

- Apple asks you to confirm it's you about once a month. When that happens, a notification and Settings ask you to sign in again; it only takes the code from your Apple device.
- App-specific passwords don't work. Use your Apple Account password.
- With Advanced Data Protection on, iCloud Drive only works outside Apple devices when **Access iCloud Data on the Web** is on. On an iPhone it's in Settings, your name, iCloud. After turning it on, sign in again and approve the request on your iPhone.

## Renaming and folders

Renaming an account in Settings renames its folder in `~/Cloud`. Kept files and files downloaded on demand take up space on this computer, so they count toward your free space; **Free up space** and removing the account give it back.
