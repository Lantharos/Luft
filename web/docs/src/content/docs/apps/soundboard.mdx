Soundboard plays sounds into games and calls. While it runs, your computer has a second microphone called **Soundboard**, which carries your voice and your sounds together, so you pick that one input and people hear both. Install it from [Schelf](/apps/schelf/).

## Getting started

1. Add sounds with **Add sounds**, or drag files onto the window. MP3, WAV, FLAC, Ogg, Opus, AAC and the sound of most video files all work. Each file is copied, so it keeps working if the original moves.
2. In your game or call, pick **Soundboard** as the microphone.
3. Click a tile.

The line above your microphone says how many apps are listening. Soundboard only opens your real microphone while an app is listening, and closes it again afterwards.

## Your microphone and what you hear

Choose which microphone passes through and how loud it is. Muting it here closes it completely, and your sounds keep playing.

**Hear sounds yourself** plays them on your speakers or headphones too, at a level of its own. Your own voice never comes back to you.

**Use as default input** makes Soundboard the microphone apps get unless they're told otherwise, and puts back the one you had when Soundboard quits. Nothing changes unless you turn this on.

## Tiles

Right-click a tile and choose **Edit** to change its name, emoji, icon or picture, colour and level, or to trim it by dragging the two handles on its waveform.

- **Loop** repeats it until you press it again.
- **Stop other sounds** cuts off whatever is playing.
- **While held** plays it only as long as you hold its hotkey.

Search finds sounds on every board. **Stop all** ends everything at once.

## Hotkeys

Open a sound with **Edit** and choose **Change** next to Hotkey. Luft's shortcut window lists all your sounds, along with one that stops everything. Hotkeys work while a game has focus, and do nothing on the lock screen. A key can be used alone if it's a function key or on the number pad; any other key needs Ctrl, Alt or Super with it.

## Boards

Group sounds into boards from the sidebar. Right-click a board to rename, export or delete it. An export is one `.soundboard` file with every sound in it, which you can send to someone or import on another computer. Hotkeys stay with the computer they were set on.

## With the window closed

Turn on **Keep running with the window closed** and your hotkeys and the Soundboard microphone stay when you close the window. The tray icon opens it again, stops all sounds, or quits. While nothing is playing it uses next to no power.
