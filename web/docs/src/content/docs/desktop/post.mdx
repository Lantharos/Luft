Post sends files, folders and text between this computer and phones, tablets and computers on the same network, and takes what they send. Everything goes straight between the devices, encrypted, without an account or the internet.

Other Luft computers have Post already. Phones and other computers need the free [LocalSend](https://localsend.org) app, which is there for Android, iPhone and iPad, Windows, macOS and Linux.

## Sending

- **From an app.** Apps such as Flatbed and Magpie have **Send…**, which opens the Send sheet. Choose a device under **Devices nearby**, or an app that opens the file.
- **From Rover.** Right-click files or folders and choose **Send to device**, then the device.
- **From the taskbar.** Post sits beside the network and sound icons. Drop files on it, or on an empty part of the taskbar, to open the Send sheet for them. Click it to see the devices nearby, choose one, and send files, a folder or text.

A device that asks for a PIN shows it on its screen; Post asks for it before sending.

## Receiving

When a device sends something, a window says who sends what and how big it is, with **Accept** and **Decline**. Once the files are on their way it shows them arriving, then where they went, with **Show in folder** and **Open**. Received files go to the Post folder in Downloads, or a folder you choose. A sender can't put them anywhere else, make them hidden or replace a file that's already there: a second `cat.jpg` arrives as `cat (2).jpg`.

Text someone sends waits in a window until you copy it. Text from your own devices goes straight to the clipboard.

## Who can find this computer

Click Post in the taskbar, or open Settings → **Post**, to choose:

- **No one**: no device can find this computer or send to it, and Post leaves the taskbar. You can still send.
- **Your devices**: only devices you added can find it and send to it, without asking first. This is how it starts.
- **Everyone**: any device nearby can find it and offer files, which wait for you to accept them.

To add a device, choose **Add a device** in Settings → Post. Send something to this computer from the other device with LocalSend, and enter the PIN Settings shows when it asks. You can also turn on **Always accept** when a device sends something while everyone can find this computer. Remove a device in Settings → Post to stop that.

Devices can only find this computer on networks marked home. On a public network, such as a café's, the firewall keeps Post closed and Post doesn't look for devices or announce itself, and Settings → Post says so, with a button to mark the network as home.

## Settings

Settings → **Post** also has:

- **Name**: what other devices call this computer. It's the computer's name unless you choose another.
- **Save received files in**: the Post folder in Downloads, or a folder you choose.
- **Put text from your devices on the clipboard**, and **Put a single picture someone sends on the clipboard too**.
