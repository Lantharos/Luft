The board turns a workspace into an endless canvas. Every window gets its own spot side by side, nothing overlaps, and you move around the canvas instead of shuffling windows. Zoom out to see everything at once, zoom into one window to work in it, and slide over to the next.

<Shot name="board" alt="A board with several apps side by side" />

## Turning it on and off

- Press <kbd>Super</kbd> twice, quickly.
- Or swipe up with four fingers on the touchpad or touchscreen; swipe down to leave.

Each workspace has its own board. When you leave, the window you were in is maximized, so you carry on where you were, and the others are minimized.

With [window tiling](/windows/tiling/) on, windows show on the board at their own size rather than their tiles' size, and tile again when you leave.

While the board is on, the taskbar steps aside. A small dock in the bottom-right corner holds Start, one icon per app, the status icons, battery and clock; clicking an app enters its next window, and clicking the clock opens Quick settings.

## Moving around

| | Keyboard | Touchpad | Touchscreen | Mouse |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Pan | <kbd>Super</kbd>+arrows | Three fingers, or scroll with two | Three fingers, or two on the empty canvas | Drag the empty canvas, or drag with the middle button |
| Zoom | <kbd>Super</kbd>+<kbd>=</kbd> and <kbd>Super</kbd>+<kbd>-</kbd> | Pinch | Pinch | <kbd>Ctrl</kbd>+scroll |
| See everything | <kbd>Super</kbd>+<kbd>0</kbd> | | | Double-click the canvas |

## Entering windows

Entering a window brings it to the front and fills the view with it, so you can work in it as usual.

- <kbd>Super</kbd>+<kbd>Enter</kbd> enters the window you're using.
- Pinch out over a window.
- When zoomed out, click a window.
- Maximizing a window on the board enters it.

While you're in a window, <kbd>Super</kbd>+arrows, or three fingers in any direction, move to the window next to it that way, as if pulling it in. <kbd>Super</kbd>+<kbd>Esc</kbd>, pinching in, or three fingers down when there's no window above step back out to the whole board. **Show all windows** in the corner does the same.

## Arranging

When zoomed out, drag a window to move it. On a touchpad, tap with three fingers and then swipe to move the window under them.

Making a window full screen sets the board aside. Leave full screen and you're back on the board, in that window.
