Click the status icons on the taskbar, or press <kbd>Super</kbd>+<kbd>S</kbd>, to open Quick settings.

## Tiles

| Tile | Does |
| --- | --- |
| Wi-Fi, Wired, Mobile, Tether | Turn each connection on or off; the arrow lists networks |
| VPN | Connect to your VPNs |
| Bluetooth | On or off, and your devices |
| Airplane Mode | Turns off every radio |
| Power Mode | Performance, Balanced or Power Saver |
| Keep Awake | Keeps the screen on until turned off, or for 30 minutes, an hour or two hours |
| Night Light | Warmer colours in the evening |
| Dark Style | Switches between light and dark |
| Do Not Disturb | Holds back notification banners |
| Keyboard Backlight | Off, low or high |
| Auto Rotate | Turns the screen with the way you hold a tablet or convertible, or keeps it still; <kbd>Super</kbd>+<kbd>O</kbd> switches it too |
| Battery | The charge and time left; opens Power settings |
| Rain | Rain on or off; the arrow picks drizzle, rain or a storm, whether it shows on the desktop, and its sound and volume |
| Screenshot, Settings, Lock, Auto Rotate, Rain | Off at first; add them if you like |

Tiles for hardware you don't have stay hidden.

## Sliders

Volume, with a menu to pick the output device, the microphone, and brightness. With several displays that can change brightness, including external monitors over DDC/CI, a menu sets each one.

## Making it yours

- **Drag** a tile to move it.
- **Right-click**, or touch and hold, a tile to open its settings or remove it.
- **Right-click empty space**, or touch and hold it, to add tiles, or reset the layout.

What's playing appears in [Notifications](/desktop/notifications/), not here.
